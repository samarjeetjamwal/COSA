Where the numbers come from

This dashboard is driven entirely by the eight modules in the left rail. Change any input — a raw milk price, a labour rate, a budget line — and the executive view recalculates immediately. That is the core discipline of cost accounting for a multi-product plant like this one: one accurate, current cost base feeding every decision, from shelf pricing to board reporting.

Module 03

Standard Costing & Variance Analysis

Classic material and labour variances, computed the way a monthly cost report needs them — split into price/rate and usage/efficiency, tagged favourable or adverse.

Direct material variance — raw milk

MPV = (Std Price − Act Price) × Act Qty  |  MUV = (Std Qty − Act Qty) × Std Price

Direct labour variance — processing & packing

LRV = (Std Rate − Act Rate) × Act Hrs  |  LEV = (Std Hrs − Act Hrs) × Std Rate
